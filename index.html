My Dearest Chanda,

I don't know who named the moon Chanda first — but whoever it was (me), they must have met some version of you. Because you are everything the moon is — soft, luminous, mysterious, and impossibly beautiful. You shine brightest in the world of darkness, and you pull me like the tide, gently and completely.

I love the way you laugh without holding back. I love the way you are. I love the way you look at a sunset, and make it feel like the world painted it just for you. You make ordinary moments extraordinary.

Every picture of you is a reminder of how lucky I am — to know you, to love you, and to be loved by you. Whether you're in a stunning saree, on top of a mountain, wearing sunglasses like a superstar, or just being your messy, beautiful, genuine self — you are always, always the most beautiful thing in the frame.

I love you, Chanda. I love you because in you, I found everything I ever wanted. You are caring, giving, idiotically and deliciously feminine, wonderfully intelligent, and wonderfully silly as well. I am totally and madly in love with your hair, eyes, voice, nose, smile, laugh, the warmth that I feel around you, and YOU.
I love you in a way that would worry a therapist and thrill a poet.

I want nothing else in this life than to be with you — to listen and watch you all the time, to admire your beautiful voice and your beauty, to argue and laugh with you, to spend my entire life with you, and to take care of you.

Every single time I think of October 10th, you walking towards me — that was the moment of my life. Everything has been better since you, and everything will be with you and only you. Because of you, I experience what true happiness is and will be for my entire life. With you, I can be delusionally optimistic.
You complete me, Chanda Chaturvedi.

By all the laws of nature and the universe, we were meant to belong to different skies— you, my gentle Chanda, pure, tranquil, and glowing in the quiet solace of the night, and I, born to be Aditya, the restless, burning light of the day.
Nature dictated that I could only ever watch You from afar. passing by like silent tides on opposite sides of the sky.
You had every reason to turn away, every reason to remain in the calm stillness of your world while I stayed in mine.

But God looked at the laws of nature and decided to break them all for us.
He knew that a sun without its Moon is just a burning flame with nowhere to rest, and a moon without her sun has no light to share. So He rewrote the cosmos.
The universe chose to rewrite its own celestial rules just so two stars from opposite worlds could finally eclipse into one—shattering the distance and destining us not just to cross paths for a fleeting moment, but to belong to each other truly, completely, passionately, undeniably together and forever.

The 'seven minutes' theory says we relive our best memories at the end. I want my whole life to be that memory, shared with you, lasting even beyond death's door. Life isn't measured by achievements, but by the person who makes your heart feel at home you are the memory that stays when everything else fades, i want every second of those seven minutes to be filled with your charming smile, your madly laugh,your moodiness and every quiet moment we've and we will ever share.

— Always yours, with all the stars 🌙✨
